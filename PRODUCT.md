# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creators turning personal photos and their own narration into emotionally engaging vertical videos without professional editing software.

## Product Purpose

Generate a grounded Draw My Life first cut, then give creators scene-level controls that preserve their identity, voice, story, and editorial intent.

## Brand Personality

Human, intimate, playful. A small creator studio, not an AI automation dashboard.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-video interfaces, glossy gradients, synthetic imagery, poster-like photo collages, corporate whiteboard explainers, and motion that only loops or changes colors.

## Design Principles

- The creator's real voice and memories are the source of truth.
- Show the drawing process, not merely the finished image.
- Familiar editing controls should disappear into the task.
- Imperfection should feel physical and intentional, never unstable.
- Every visual change should support the spoken story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operation. Captions remain editable and legible. Respect reduced-motion preferences in the editor.
